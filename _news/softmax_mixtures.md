Our new paper [Near-Purity Screening and Dominant-Atom Estimation in Softmax Mixtures and Their Ensembles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12647) is available on arXiv. It introduces a criterion for screening near-pure softmax mixtures without knowing the number of components and establishes finite-sample guarantees for estimating dominant and shared mixture atoms.
