Our new paper [Federated Independent Component Analysis via Spectral Alignment and Robust Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.20532) is available on arXiv. It combines spectral alignment with robust aggregation to estimate a shared ICA mixing matrix across heterogeneous clients, with near-minimax guarantees in the homogeneous setting.
