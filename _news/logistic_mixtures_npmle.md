Our new paper [Nonparametric Maximum Likelihood Estimation for Mixtures of Logistic Regressions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14445) is available on arXiv. It establishes nonasymptotic convergence rates for the sieve NPMLE in mixtures of logistic regressions and provides a finite-particle algorithm with provable computational guarantees.
