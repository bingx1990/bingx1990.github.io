Our new paper [Convergence and Optimality of the EM Algorithm Under Multi-Component Gaussian Mixture Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08237) has been accepted by *Biometrika*, 2026+. We establish the minimax optimal rate of convergence of the EM algorithm for multi-component GMMs in full generality.
