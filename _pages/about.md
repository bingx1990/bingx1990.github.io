Xin is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He is currently on leave from his position in the Department of Statistical Sciences at the University of Toronto, where he has been an Assistant Professor since 2022.

He finished his Ph.D. in Statistics in June, 2021 from the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Cornell University, advised jointly by [Florentina Bunea](https://bunea.stat.cornell.edu) and [Marten Wegkamp](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~marten/). Before joining the University of Toronto, Xin had a 3-month visit at the Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics at the University of Cambridge hosted by [Richard J. Samworth](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~rjs57/). Prior to his Ph.D. study, he received a BS in Mathematics in 2013 from Shandong University, China and an MS in Statistics in 2016 from University of Washington, Seattle.

Xin grew up in Zibo, Shandong Province, China, which became a national barbecue sensation in 2023. He enjoys sports and working out, with basketball a particular favorite. He also loves cooking Chinese food, a skill honed in Ithaca and during the pandemic through his own program of trial, error, and dinner.

He shares his life with his lovely wife, [Yaosheng Xu](https://yaoshengxu.github.io), and their adorable dog, Luca.
